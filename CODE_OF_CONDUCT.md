# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing experience, hardware, and technical approaches.
- Give actionable feedback and discuss evidence rather than attacking people.
- Clearly distinguish personal observations, hypotheses, and reproduced results.
- Protect privacy: do not publish secrets, private URLs, corporate information, or identifying workstation details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of sensitive information, and knowingly misleading benchmark or compatibility claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
