# Truncated-output policy

Treat truncated or incomplete output as incomplete evidence.

- Do not infer that omitted files, lines, definitions, references, or findings do not exist.
- Use a targeted read, narrower search, pagination, or another appropriate tool to obtain the missing portion.
- Preserve the distinction between text that was observed and text that was not available.
- If the missing content cannot be retrieved, label conclusions that depend on it **Unverified** and describe the limitation.
