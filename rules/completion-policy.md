# Completion policy

Do not claim a task is complete until its requested outcomes have been checked.

1. Re-read the requested requirements and compare them with the actual result.
2. Run the smallest relevant test, check, or inspection available.
3. Report what was completed, what was verified, and what remains unverified.
4. Do not claim a command succeeded unless its result was observed.
5. If a check could not run, state why and avoid a success-shaped fallback.
6. Stop when the agreed scope is satisfied; do not make unrelated changes.
